# Forever Countdown

This addon's spec. The shared rules in `../CLAUDE.md` come first: they hold every rule that isn't this addon's own and win on any conflict.

@../CLAUDE.md

A panel in the objective tracker's style that counts down to World of Warcraft Forever's launch, with the stress test, the beta's end and name reservation on the way. Agreed with the user on the design canvas (board Z9, with the "Forever launches in" minimized bar). Design canvas, for mockups the user wants: https://claude.ai/artifact/TP8ZFtWEZYej4VEp7DwVKh

- The name keeps "Forever", an exception to CurseForge's naming rule in the shared rules (the user's choice): here it names the launch counted down to, not a game or version label, and the CurseForge description makes that clear.
- Folder, repo and packages `ForeverCountdown`. GitHub `tembugi/ForeverCountdown`, public (the user's word, for CurseForge's packager). CurseForge project ID 1727790, https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/forever-countdown (category Miscellaneous); the repo's webhook is in place, so a pushed tag publishes.

## Dates
From Blizzard's announcements, in `Countdown.lua`. Everything the player sees is in their own time, computed from the official times, and everything the game itself provides comes from the game (the user asked):
- Launch: November 4, 2026 at 3:00 p.m. PST (UTC-8), so 23:00 UTC, one moment the world over. The clock counts to it with `GetServerTime()` (the realm's clock, right even when the computer's is off).
- The launch line shows that moment in the player's time zone the way the game writes a date and a time: its full date `FULLDATE` with its month and weekday names (`FULLDATE_MONTH_*`, `WEEKDAY_*`, in the player's language and order) and the clock's `GameTime_GetFormattedTime`, which follows the 12/24-hour setting as the clock by the minimap does. Helsinki sees "Thursday, November 5 2026, 01:00", California "Wednesday, November 4 2026, 3:00 PM".
- The player's time zone comes from `date`, as the game's own clock gets local time (`GameTime_GetLocalTime`); the game has no other source for it.
- From Blizzard's posts of October 20, 2026 (the user asked for the times of day, 1.2.0):
  - Beta ends: October 23 at 10:00 a.m. PDT, 17:00 UTC (it was October 21, date only).
  - Stress test (its own row, the user asked): October 21, 21:00 to 01:00 UTC in the Americas, Oceania, Korea and Taiwan (2:00 to 6:00 p.m. PDT); in Europe 17:00 to 01:00 UTC (19:00 to 03:00 CEST, as Blizzard's table has it). "Stress test starts" until its start, "Stress test ends" until its end, then "Stress test ended", greyed.
  - Name reservation starts October 27 at 1:00 p.m. PDT in North America, 18:00 CET in Europe, October 28 at 07:00 KST in Korea and 07:00 CST in Taiwan; it ends November 3 (no time given). "Name reservation starts" until its start, then "Name reservation ends" with November 3.
  - The stress test ends at 01:00 UTC everywhere, so only its start differs by region.
  - Start times that differ by region show for the realm's region (`GetCurrentRegionName`). The beta has none (see Behavior), so there each region gets its own line under the title, earliest first: "Europe: October 27, 7:00 PM · in 6 Days" (the game's `EUROPE`, `KOREA`, `TAIWAN`; "Americas & Oceania" is the addon's own, the game has no name for it). Once a region's time has come, its line is greyed and says so in the past tense: "Europe: started October 21 · Today" (the user asked). When every region has started, the title turns to "ends" with one line. Blizzard's code shows only "US" and "CN"; "EU", "KR" and "TW" are assumed until seen in game. A named region with no announced time (China) shows the date only.
- A milestone with a time of day shows its local date and time ("October 23, 20:00 · in 2 Days", the time from `GameTime_GetFormattedTime`) until it passes, then its date only ("October 23 · Today"). Under a day before it, the line is only a countdown to the second: "in 04:12:33", "Europe: in 04:12:33" (the user asked; with the date and time too it was too busy in game). The panel's width is measured with zeros in the countdown, so it doesn't change every second.
- Beta began (September 17, 2026), name reservation's end, and the regional ones without a time have only a Pacific date, so they can't become a local moment: the date shows as announced, and days are counted on the player's own calendar, so on that date the line says "Today" wherever the player is. (With Pacific days, "Today" started at 10:00 in Finland.) Days show as the game's event scheduler writes a day (`EVENT_SCHEDULER_DAY_FORMAT`: "October 21").
- The game holds these dates nowhere an addon can read: no API returns time events (only the `TimeEventFlag` enum, with `GlobalLaunch`), the `TimeEvent` table isn't in this build, and the holiday tables have nothing for Forever (checked on build 1.60.1.70205).
- A date or time that changes needs a new release. When Blizzard announces a time for name reservation's end, ask the user whether to show it too.

## Look
The tracker's own pieces, read from the game while it runs, with Blizzard's values (from `ObjectiveTrackerModuleHeaderTemplate`) only as fallbacks: the section header's width, height, title offset and button size come from the quest section's header (`QuestObjectiveTracker.Header`).
- Spacing between the header, the milestones and their lines: 8, 8 and 2 units, the mockup's, a little tighter than the quest tracker's own (10, 10 and 4). The user kept it after the audit showed the difference, so it stays rather than being read from the tracker.
- Width: at least a tracker section's 260, and wide enough for the longest line (the header with its clock and button, or any row's title or line), measured from the texts once they are made. The same width open and minimized (the user asked).
- The header is 26 tall with `UI-QuestTracker-Secondary-Objective-Header` art (at its own 300 width, centered, or stretched to the panel when the panel is wider at large Text Size) and the tracker's own section minimize button (`ObjectiveTrackerModuleMinimizeButtonTemplate`; made with its values when the tracker isn't loaded), its art switched as the tracker's header does (`UI-QuestTrackerButton-Secondary-Collapse`/`-Expand`, yellow highlight). It reads "Countdown to" (gold) then "Forever", both in the header font.
- Fonts: the tracker's own font objects as they are, `ObjectiveTrackerHeaderFont` for every header word and `ObjectiveTrackerLineFont` for the rows, so they follow its Text Size setting (Edit Mode swaps the font behind both; the user asked for the tracker's sizes). The rows stack by their text's height, and a hook on `ObjectiveTrackerManager.SetTextSize` re-measures the clock, the width and the height on the next frame. Colors from `OBJECTIVE_TRACKER_COLOR` (Normal for lines, Complete for what is behind). Titles and the header's gold words use the game's bright gold, `NORMAL_FONT_COLOR` (the tracker's highlight gold): its resting Header gold read too dim in game (the user).
- Every "Forever" is white with a pale-blue glow that rises and fades (the word in pale blue a pixel out in eight directions behind it; the game's outlines are always black). A gleam sweeping across the word was dropped after the user saw it in game.
- Rows, as quests show in the tracker: a marker on the left, a title, and a dashed line under it (`QUEST_DASH`). What is behind is greyed (Complete).
  - "!" (the game's `QuestNormal` art), greyed and still: Beta began.
  - Blue "!" (`QuestDaily`, the daily quest's marker, for a one-day event; the user's pick), shown at 16 like the "?" (both 32 in the atlas): Stress test. Greyed like Beta began once the test is over, and it stays (the user's choice).
  - "?" (`QuestTurnin`): Beta ends. It wiggles from side to side on its base for attention, then rests, as a plain-Lua Rotation animation group (the user asked for a wiggle, not a bounce; turning the texture with `SetRotation` didn't show in game). It stops once the beta is over.
  - A quill drawn in gold, writing a line of ink: Name reservation. The game has no quill art; the quill is drawn from discs of the game's round soft texture (`Interface\CharacterFrame\TempPortraitAlphaMask`), unsnapped.
  - The infinity sign as a calligraphic silver ribbon (thick across a slanted nib, thin along it, dark edge, one strand over the other at the crossing): Forever launches. It is `Infinity.tga` (128 x 64), drawn by `Art/make_art.py` (shape A, the user's pick from real-size previews), shown 24 units wide centered in the icon column. Drawn in game from Line objects it never showed, and from ~200 small discs it wobbled. A light slides along it: a section of the ribbon lighting up (additive discs as wide as the ribbon over a faint halo 2.4 wider, brightest mid-section, fading at both ends, dimmed where it passes under the crossing); a single white disc read as a ball.
- The clock sits in the header after "Countdown to Forever", centered on it, 8 units after "Forever", open and minimized (the user asked). "Forever launches" has the launch date and time under it (see Dates).
- The clock: days, hours, minutes and seconds in bold white, the tracker's header font with a black outline, 3 larger than the header text, with gold colons 4 larger than the figures, raised so their dots sit at the figures' middle, that stay still. Each pair of figures and each colon is its own self-sizing text, each following the one before at a 1-unit gap; the game places them by their drawn size (the user asked for even gaps and readable colons). Widths the addon measured itself came out wrong in game and spread the clock apart, so no clock text is given a width. The clock is centered on the title (the user's choice; a shared baseline and aligned tops were tried and dropped). The seconds fade in as they tick. Minimizing fades the rows (alpha) instead of hiding them.
- Days use the game's `D_DAYS`: an upcoming date reads "October 27 · in 22 Days" ("in %s" is the addon's own; the game has no such string; the user asked); "Today" on the day itself, the word the game's calendar writes for an event on the current day (`COMMUNITIES_CALENDAR_TODAY`); a date that has passed reads "September 17 · 18 Days ago" with the game's "%s ago" (`CURRENCY_TRANSFER_LOG_TIME_FORMAT`; the user asked for it on Beta began).
- Minimized (the minimize button, which clicks like the tracker's own, `SOUNDKIT.IG_MAINMENU_OPTION_CHECKBOX_ON`): only the header, "Countdown to Forever" and the clock, as when open (the user chose this over "Forever launches in").
- Once Forever has launched (board Z11 A, the user's pick): the clock stays at 00:00:00:00 (white, gold colons); every milestone is behind and greyed like "Beta began", in the past tense with how many days ago ("Name reservation ended", "Forever launched"); the launch line becomes the launch's own date in the player's time zone with how many days ago ("November 5 · 2 Days ago" in Helsinki); its "Forever" turns grey without the glow, its infinity sign greyed and still, the quill at rest with its line written, greyed. A last line follows, "Welcome to Forever!" (gold, the shining white "Forever"), with no line under it and the lit infinity sign beside it, its light running.
- Tried on the canvas and rejected: unit labels under the clock, progress bars, paw prints, window borders, close buttons and stamps.

## Behavior
- The panel can be dragged by its header anywhere on the screen; the place is saved. Until it is moved, it mirrors the objective tracker on the left of the screen: as far in from the left edge as the tracker is from the right, level with its top, read from the tracker's place in the game and followed once a second (the user asked; left of the minimap it covered the default buffs). Before the tracker has a place, Blizzard's preset one (110 in, 275 down).
- One OnUpdate drives everything that moves, and the game runs it only while the panel is shown. The texts change once a second. Nothing else runs. It sets only what changes from frame to frame (the light's sizes and brightness, the ink), and minimized only the header's glow and the clock; a test holds it to that (about 89 calls a frame open in the stand-in, down from 162).
- No slash commands, options or tooltips (none asked for).
- Chat only when something stopped working: if loading, the animation driver or the relayout fails, its error goes to the game's error handler, the panel stops updating (a panel the load left half built is hidden), and chat says so once.
- Loading runs on the addon's own frame's `ADDON_LOADED` and `PLAYER_LOGIN`, never `EventUtil`: its callback registry takes owner IDs from a counter (`CreateCounter`) shared with Blizzard's code, and registering from the addon tainted it, so the game's settings and gamepad action bars read it tainted at login (taint log, 1.2.0).
- On the beta the game knows no region: `GetCurrentRegionName()` is "", `GetCurrentRegion()` 110, the `portal` CVar "test" (seen in game, 1.2.0).

## Tests
`Tests/run.lua` tests the rules (`Countdown.lua`) and runs the panel in a stand-in for the game (`Tests/standin.lua`: the tracker's fonts, header and Text Size, timers and the clock). Players in time zones from UTC-10 to UTC+13 are checked with a stand-in for `date` at fixed offsets, the game's own formatter copied from `GameTimeUtil.lua`, and its numbered placeholders in `format`. The launched panel can't be seen in game before November 4, so the stand-in runs it across the launch. The user saw the launched panel in game in a preview build whose clock ran ahead to the launch (branch launch-preview, since deleted); the real build at the real launch matched it widget for widget in the stand-in, and a test holds the panel's text at that moment to what the user saw, with the rows and dates changed since (1.2.0).

## Saved data
`ForeverCountdownDB` (account-wide): the save format, whether the panel is minimized, and where it was moved (a point of the panel on the same point of the screen, and an offset).

## Logo
The infinity sign standing as an hourglass, white (shaded to soft silver), drawn with a broad pen like the addon's own infinity sign, gold sand (the top bulb as full as D2's) falling from the top bulb through the crossing in a straight stream onto a heap below (the stream from D3 in place of grains, the user asked), inside the gold ring of the other addons' logos at D4's size, on their dark ground (board L4, D5, the user's pick). The dark seam where one strand passes over the other shows only where the strands overlap (the user marked it running into the sand). Tried and dropped: the sign lying on its side as the hourglass (L1), glass bulbs, rings and end plates, gold instead of white.
